What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of getting a company named, described accurately and cited in answers from AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews. It combines third-party coverage, answer-ready content and technical signals such as structured data.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO ranks pages in a list of links. GEO earns a place inside the answer itself. Good SEO helps, but AI engines lean heavily on what credible third parties say about you, so coverage matters more for GEO than it does for classic search.

How long until AI engines cite us?

Technical and content fixes can show up within weeks as engines recrawl. Citations that come from new coverage build over a few months. I track a fixed set of buyer questions every month so you can see the answer change.

Which AI engines do you track?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot.

Can you guarantee AI citations?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. AI answers change constantly. I show you exactly where the gaps are, do the work most likely to close them and report what moves.
