Questions

What founders ask first.

What does a B2B tech PR agency do?

A B2B tech PR agency gets your company covered by the reporters your buyers and investors read. That means building the story, pitching it to the right journalists, preparing your executives for interviews and turning coverage into credibility you can use in sales and fundraising.

How is AI search visibility different from SEO?

SEO works to rank your pages in search results. AI search visibility, also called generative engine optimization (GEO), works to get your company named and cited inside AI-generated answers. The technical basics overlap, but AI citations depend heavily on third-party coverage, which is why PR and GEO belong together.

How much does PR cost?

Every engagement is scoped to your goals and timeline, so there's no rate card. On a free 30-minute consult I'll tell you what it takes to hit your goals and what that costs.

Do you work with hardware and physical AI companies?

Yes. Robotics, devices, data center and industrial AI companies are a core focus. I have years of experience pitching hardware, manufacturing and construction stories to business and trade press.
