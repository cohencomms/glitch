The problem

Every AI company sounds the same to a reporter.

Reporters get dozens of AI pitches a day. Most promise transformation and explain nothing. The companies that get covered show what the product does, prove it and give the reporter a reason to care this week.

What gets ignored

  • Claims with no benchmark or customer behind them
  • "AI-powered" as the whole story
  • Launch news with no timing hook

What gets covered

  • A specific capability a reporter can test or verify
  • A customer result with real numbers
  • A clear point of view on where the market is going

Questions

What does an AI PR agency do?

An AI PR agency gets AI companies covered accurately by the reporters who shape their market. That covers launches, model releases, funding news, technical briefings and founder visibility, plus making sure the coverage feeds how AI engines describe you.

Do you work with physical AI and hardware companies?

Yes. Robotics, edge devices, data center and industrial AI are a core focus, backed by years of hardware, manufacturing and construction press work.

How do you handle technical claims?

I work from your benchmarks and documentation, pressure-test claims before they go out and pitch only what a reporter can verify. It protects your credibility, and mine.
