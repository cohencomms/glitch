Questions

Startup PR, answered.

When should a startup hire a PR firm?

When you have news worth covering in the next three to six months: a launch, a raise, a major customer or a category story. PR works best with a few months of runway before the moment that matters.

Should a startup hire a PR firm or a consultant?

It depends on who does the work. Many firms pitch you with a senior partner and staff the account with junior people. A senior consultant costs less in overhead and you get experience on every task. Ask any firm who will write your pitches and call reporters.

How fast will we get coverage?

Most clients see first coverage within the first one to two months, depending on the news and timing. Strong, timely news moves faster.

Do you guarantee coverage?

No. Earned media can't be guaranteed honestly, and anyone who promises it is usually selling paid placement. I'll tell you upfront what's likely and why.

What should we have ready before starting?

A clear sense of your next milestone, someone available for interviews and any customer results you can share. I'll build the story from there.
