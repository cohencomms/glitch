Questions

About the audit.

How is this different from a free AI visibility checker?

Checker tools count mentions. The audit explains why engines answer the way they do, which sources drive it and what to change, including the coverage you'd need to earn. You get judgment, not just a score.

What do you need from me?

A 20-minute intake call, your main competitors and the questions your buyers ask before they buy. I handle the rest.

Do I need to sign up for a retainer afterward?

No. The report is yours to act on however you like. Many clients ask me to run the plan, but that's a separate decision.

How much does the audit cost?

It's a fixed fee, quoted on the intake call based on how many products and markets we cover. No surprises after that.
